ArcRise — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 June 2026 · Last updated: 30 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the ArcRise game ("the App", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By playing ArcRise you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We keep data to a minimum. We collect:

  • Anonymous account identifier. A randomly generated ID created on your device the first time you play, used to save your progress and your online identity. It is not linked to your real-world identity.
  • Profile you choose. The username and avatar you pick in the game.
  • Gameplay data. Your scores, distances, the path/trail you draw, and friend connections you create.
  • Local settings. Sound, music, mode and similar preferences (stored only on your device).

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos from your device library beyond an avatar you intentionally pick, or your contacts.

2. How we use it

  • To run core features: leaderboards, your profile shown to friends, cross-player "ghost" trails, and on-map markers.
  • To save and restore your progress across sessions on the same device.

Your chosen username is public on leaderboards and visible to other players. Please do not use personal information as your username.

3. Where your data is stored

Online data (anonymous ID, username, avatar, scores, distances, trail, friend connections) is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Anonymous Authentication). Google processes this data as a service provider; see Google's Firebase privacy and Google Privacy Policy. Device settings are stored locally in your browser/app storage.

4. Advertising and payments

This version contains no third-party advertising and no active in-app purchases. If these are introduced later, this policy and any required consent prompts will be updated beforehand.

5. Your choices and data deletion

You can permanently delete all of your data — both on your device and online (your leaderboard scores, profile, and friend connections) — at any time from inside the App: Settings → Delete Account & Data. This action is immediate and cannot be undone. You may also request deletion by emailing us (see Contact).

6. Data retention

Online data is retained until you delete it via the in-app option or by request. Local data is removed when you delete your data in the App or uninstall it.

7. Children

ArcRise does not require any personal information to play and is not directed at collecting data from children. No real identity is needed to use the App.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
